English Language Teaching

English Language Teaching (ELT) plays a crucial role in modern educational institutions by enabling students to communicate globally, access knowledge, and participate in diverse cultures. Effective ELT methodology focuses on interactive, student-centered approaches, including communicative language teaching, task-based learning, and the integration of technology. These methods develop speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills in a balanced way. By adapting teaching strategies to learner's needs, educators can improve engagement and ensure meaningful language acquisition in both academic and real-life contexts.
